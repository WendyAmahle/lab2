Latest Project

As part of my academic journey at the University of the Witwatersrand, I participated in a collaborative group project where we developed a mobile application for an anonymous counselling system. The purpose of the application was to provide a secure and confidential platform for individuals facing personal, emotional, or psychological challenges who may be hesitant to seek traditional counselling services due to fear of stigma or exposure. The system was designed to protect user anonymity while enabling safe and reliable communication between users and counsellors with strong consideration given to data privacy, secure authentication, and the protection of sensitive information. Throughout the project, I contributed to the system design, feature development, and testing processes, while working closely with my team to ensure the application was user-friendly, accessible, and ethically responsible. This experience strengthened my technical skills in software development, enhanced my teamwork and problem-solving abilities, and deepened my understanding of the importance of security and confidentiality in applications that handle sensitive user data. .
